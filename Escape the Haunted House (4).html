Escape the Haunted House

English class with Margarete Trigueiro de Lima

How to play

You and your team are locked inside an escape room. Clear all 6 rooms before the candle burns out.

Steps

  1. Read the story of the room.
  2. Click the objects in the room. Each object has a question.
  3. Talk with your team and use English! Press 🔊 to listen to the question.
  4. Press “Check”. Green means correct. Red means try again.
  5. Solve all the objects to open the door. Each room gives you one secret letter.
  6. Write the letters down. They are out of order! In the last room, put them in order to make the secret word.

Rules

  • You have 15 minutes. The timer stops while this window is open.
  • Stuck? Press “Get a hint”. Hints are counted at the end.
  • Capital letters do not matter. Write the full verb, for example: is cooking.
  • You cannot skip a room.
  • Use the Sound button in the footer to turn the sounds on or off.

The 6 rooms

  • The Hall: pronouns
  • The Library: adverbs of frequency
  • The Kitchen: present continuous
  • The Attic: prepositions of time (in, on, at)
  • The Cellar: the clock
  • The Crypt: final review and the secret word
